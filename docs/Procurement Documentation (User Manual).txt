Introduction
Purpose

This user manual is a comprehensive reference for individuals and organizations using the Fortune Procurement System. Its primary goal is to ensure users at all levels—administrators, employees, warehouse staff, procurement officers, approvers, suppliers, and TSQA reviewers—can effectively navigate, operate, and optimize the system's implemented functionalities.
Purchase Request (PR1) creation and lifecycle management
Warehouse stock validation
Multi-step approval workflows (PR1, PR2, PO)
Canvassing and Request for Quotation (RFQ)
Purchase Order (PO) generation and supplier acknowledgment
Delivery tracking and Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
Supplier accreditation and product catalog management
Raw Material Scientific Evaluation (RSE) and TSQA review
Internal messaging, notifications, and audit logging
The document outlines step-by-step procedures, role-based access capabilities, system workflows, and navigation guidance based solely on verified application code and database schema. It does not describe features that are not implemented in the current system.
System Scope
The Fortune Procurement System is a centralized digital workspace for managing organizational procurement processes. It is modular and role-specific. The following modules are implemented and verified in the codebase:
Module / Feature
Description
PR1 — Purchase Request
Employees create purchase requisitions with line items, purpose, required date, and priority. PR1s progress through warehouse validation and approval before canvassing.
Warehouse Validation
Warehouse staff validate stock-on-hand per line item, record availability decisions (sufficient/insufficient), and route items for internal fulfillment or procurement.
Approval Workflows
Configurable multi-step approval chains for PR1 (2 steps), PR2 Phase 1 (4 steps), PR2 Phase 2 (3 steps), and PO (4 steps). Each step requires a specific role and position.
Canvassing / RFQ
Procurement issues RFQs to invited suppliers. Suppliers submit item-level quotes including alternatives. Procurement selects winning quotes per item.
Substitute Review
Employees review and accept or reject alternative items quoted by suppliers during canvassing.
PR2 — Purchase Request v2
Procurement-generated purchase request derived from approved PR1 and completed RFQ. Requires dual-phase approval before PO generation.
Purchase Orders
Formal orders issued to selected suppliers. Include line items, pricing, delivery address, payment terms. Require multi-step approval ending with supplier acknowledgment.
Delivery Tracking
Tracks shipment status from pending through delivered. Supports delivery receipt document upload.
Goods Receipt (GRN)
Warehouse records received quantities per PO line item. GRN status: open or closed.
Supplier Accreditation
Suppliers submit accreditation applications with supporting documents. Procurement reviews and approves or rejects.
Product Catalog
Suppliers maintain product records linked to accreditation. Products pass procurement review and TSQA verification for raw materials.
RSE / TSQA Review
Raw Material Scientific Evaluation records are created for qualifying products. TSQA staff conduct scientific reviews with pass/fail results.
User & Access Management
Administrators manage users, roles, positions, departments, workflow steps, and per-position module visibility.
Notifications
In-app notification bell with read/unread tracking for workflow events.
Messaging
One-to-one internal messaging with optional file attachments.
BugTrack
Internal bug reporting with severity levels and email notifications to configured address.
Audit Logging
Immutable system action trail viewable by administrators.
Reporting / Dashboards
Role-specific dashboards with KPI counts and work queues. No standalone exportable report module exists.


Getting Started
How to Access
The Fortune Procurement System is accessed through a secure web-based login portal. Users must be provided with a valid email account before accessing any procurement functions.
To log in:
Open your web browser.
Navigate to the Fortune Procurement System URL provided by your administrator.
Enter your registered email address in the Email Address field.
Enter your account password in the Password field.
(Optional) Enable Remember this device for 30 days if using a trusted company device.
Click Sign In.

Note: Access to system modules is determined by the user's assigned role and position. Users will only see procurement functions that have been authorized for their account.
Security Reminder: Do not share login credentials with other users. Always log out when using a shared or public computer.



User Roles & Access Levels
The Fortune Procurement System uses role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure that users only have access to the functions and information required for their responsibilities. Access permissions are assigned by administrators and may be further restricted based on workflow position, department, or approval authority.
User Role
Access Privileges
Administrator
Full system access. Manage users, roles, departments, workflow configurations, approval matrices, supplier records, notifications, audit logs, and system settings.
Employee/Requestor
Create and submit Purchase Requests (PR1), monitor request status, review supplier alternative items, receive notifications, and view procurement history related to submitted requests.
Warehouse Personnel
Review submitted PR1 requests, validate stock availability, mark items as sufficient or insufficient, record inventory validation results, and route requests for further processing.
Approver
Review and approve or reject procurement documents based on assigned approval workflows. Access may include PR1 approvals, PR2 Phase 1 approvals, PR2 Phase 2 approvals, and Purchase Order approvals.
Procurement Staff
Manage RFQs, invite suppliers, review quotations, compare bids, create PR2 records, generate Purchase Orders, track deliveries, manage supplier communications, and monitor procurement activities.
Supplier
Maintain company profile, submit accreditation applications, manage product catalogs, respond to RFQs, submit quotations, acknowledge Purchase Orders, and track procurement transactions.
TSQA Reviewer
Review Raw Material Scientific Evaluation (RSE) submissions, evaluate supplier products, approve or reject scientific assessments, and provide quality assurance recommendations.
