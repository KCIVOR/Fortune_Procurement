# Procurement Workflow — What Changed

**Prepared for:** Client review
**Date:** 2026-08-04

---

**1. PR2 Certification approvers**
From: Department Head + Procurement Staff + Procurement Manager + Director (4 approvers)
Into: Procurement Staff + Procurement Manager + Director (Department Head removed)

**2. Second certification round**
From: A second full approval round (Procurement Staff → Procurement Manager → Director) after the first, before a Purchase Order could be made
Into: No second round — first certification approval goes straight to Purchase Order

**3. Stock check timing on the initial request (PR1)**
From: Warehouse checked stock last, after Supervisor and Department Head approved
Into: Warehouse checks stock first, right after the request is raised, before Supervisor and Department Head

**4. Engineering department**
From: Assumed to follow the same approval path as every other department
Into: Documented as its own path — two Department Heads sign in sequence, plus an extra approver ("ODM") after the stock check

**5. Goods requests — certification vs. supplier quotation order**
From: Supplier quotation (RFQ) could start before internal certification was approved
Into: Internal certification (PR2) is always approved first; supplier quotation only starts after that

**6. Sending the Purchase Order to the supplier**
From: Manual send by Procurement, after Finance Director approval
Into: Unchanged — still manual, never automatic

**7. Goods Receiving (GRN)**
From: Basic receipt confirmation only
Into: Now also captures an invoice number and can flag individual items for quality inspection before the record closes

---

**Needs your confirmation:** Right now, if any approver requests a revision, the request restarts from the very first step. The approved workflow document says it should only go back one step and re-enter the same stage. Which one do you want as final?
