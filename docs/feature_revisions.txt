Procurement Workflow Revision Report
Project
Procurement Management System (PR1 → GRN Workflow)
Date
June 2026

I. Current Workflow (Before Change)
The existing procurement workflow follows a warehouse-first validation approach, where requests are evaluated for stock availability before management approval is obtained.
Workflow:
Employee
→ PR1 Creation
→ Warehouse Validation
→ PR1 Approval Routing
→ Canvassing / RFQ
→ PR2
→ PR2 Approval Phase 1
→ PR2 Approval Phase 2
→ PO Generation
→ PO Approval Routing
→ Supplier PO Acknowledgment
→ Delivery Tracking
→ GRN
→ Completed
Under this process, warehouse personnel determine whether procurement activities are required before the request enters the approval process. PR2 approvals are split into two separate approval phases, and substitute item handling is not explicitly controlled through requestor approval.

II. Revised Workflow (After Change)
The revised procurement workflow introduces an approval-first approach, ensuring that management approval is obtained before warehouse verification and procurement activities begin.
Workflow:
Employee
→ PR1 Creation
→ PR1 Approval Routing
→ Warehouse Verification
→ Canvassing / RFQ
→ Supplier Quotation Submission
→ Requestor Approval of Substitute Items (If Applicable)
→ Supplier Evaluation and Awarding
→ PR2 Generation
→ PR2 Approval Routing
→ PO Generation
→ PO Approval Routing
→ Supplier PO Acknowledgment
→ Delivery Tracking
→ GRN Generation
→ TSQA Notice (Raw Materials Only)
→ Completed
This revised structure strengthens management oversight, simplifies approval routing, formalizes substitute-item approval controls, and improves procurement governance while maintaining operational efficiency.
For raw material items, the system will display an informational TSQA notice after GRN processing. No TSQA workflow, approval routing, queue, or status will be implemented.

III. Detailed Workflow Changes
Change 1: PR1 Approval Moved Before Warehouse Verification
Previous Process
Submitted PR1 requests were routed directly to Warehouse Validation. Warehouse personnel reviewed stock availability and determined whether procurement activities were required before the request entered the approval process.
Workflow:
Employee
→ Warehouse Validation
→ PR1 Approval
Revised Process
All PR1 requests must now pass the designated approval hierarchy before warehouse review begins.
Workflow:
Employee
→ PR1 Approval
→ Warehouse Verification
Business Impact
This change establishes management approval as the first control point in the procurement process. Warehouse personnel will only review requests that have already been approved by the appropriate signatories, reducing unnecessary validation work and ensuring alignment with organizational approval requirements.
Development Impact
Update PR1 submission logic:
Current:
draft → pending_warehouse
Revised:
draft → pending_approval
Warehouse verification screens and actions must remain inaccessible until final PR1 approval is completed.

Change 2: PR1 Approval Becomes Mandatory
Previous Process
PR1 approval was only triggered when Warehouse Validation identified insufficient stock requiring procurement.
Revised Process
Every submitted PR1 must pass through approval routing regardless of stock availability.
Approval Routing:
Supervisor
→ Department Head
Business Impact
This standardizes governance across all procurement requests and provides management visibility into every request before warehouse or procurement resources are utilized.
Development Impact
Remove conditional approval creation.
Automatically create:
PR1_APPROVAL
whenever a PR1 is submitted.

Change 3: Warehouse Verification Repositioned Within the Workflow
Previous Process
Warehouse Validation served as the first operational review stage after PR1 submission.
Revised Process
Warehouse Verification now occurs only after completion of PR1 approval.
Workflow:
PR1 Approval Complete
→ Warehouse Verification
Business Impact
Warehouse personnel focus exclusively on approved requests, improving efficiency and reducing unnecessary operational effort.
Development Impact
Update workflow routing and permissions to ensure Warehouse Verification is only available after final approval.

Change 4: Formal Supplier Quotation Submission Stage Added
Previous Process
Supplier quotation activities were considered part of the RFQ process but were not explicitly represented as a separate workflow stage.
Revised Process
Supplier Quotation Submission becomes a distinct stage following RFQ issuance.
Workflow:
RFQ Issued
→ Supplier Quotation Submission
Business Impact
This provides clearer visibility into supplier participation and quotation management activities.
Development Impact
Add a dedicated quotation submission stage and tracking mechanism within the RFQ process.

Change 5: Requestor Approval Required for Substitute Items
Previous Process
Procurement could evaluate supplier quotations and alternative items without a formal requestor approval gate.
Revised Process
When suppliers propose substitute or alternative items, the original requestor must review and approve the substitute item before Procurement may select it as the winning quotation.
Workflow:
Supplier Submits Substitute Item
→ Requestor Review
→ Approve or Reject
→ Procurement Awarding
Business Impact
This ensures that substitute products or services remain aligned with the requestor's operational requirements and prevents unauthorized substitutions.
Development Impact
Implement:
Requestor notification process
Substitute item approval tracking
Award restrictions for unapproved substitute items

Change 6: Supplier Evaluation and Awarding Formalized
Previous Process
Supplier evaluation and awarding occurred as part of Procurement's internal RFQ activities.
Revised Process
Supplier Evaluation and Awarding becomes an explicit workflow stage following quotation collection and substitute item approval.
Business Impact
This provides better procurement traceability and clearer supplier selection governance.
Development Impact
Introduce supplier awarding records and workflow visibility for supplier evaluation activities.

Change 8: PO Approval Routing Updated
Previous Process
Purchase Orders followed:
Buyer

Previous Process
After GRN closure, procurement requests were immediately completed.
Workflow:

IV. New End-to-End Workflow
The revised procurement workflow follows a structured approval-first approach, ensuring that management approval is obtained before warehouse verification and procurement activities begin. The following sequence describes the complete lifecycle of a procurement request from creation through completion.
Step 1: PR1 Creation and Submission
The process begins when an Employee creates and submits a Purchase Requisition Form (PR1). The request contains the required procurement details, including requested goods or services, quantity requirements, business justification, department information, and supporting attachments where applicable.
Upon submission, the PR1 automatically enters the approval workflow.

Step 2: PR1 Approval Routing
The PR1 is routed through the designated approval hierarchy before any warehouse activity takes place.
Approval Sequence:
Supervisor
→ Department Head
Only fully approved PR1 requests may proceed to Warehouse Verification.

Step 3: Warehouse Verification
Warehouse personnel review stock availability and determine procurement-required quantities.
If procurement activity is required, the request proceeds to RFQ.
For service requests, warehouse stock verification may be bypassed where inventory validation is not applicable.

Step 4: Canvassing and RFQ
Procurement creates an RFQ and invites qualified suppliers to participate.

Step 5: Supplier Quotation Submission
Suppliers submit quotations, product proposals, service proposals, specifications, and supporting documents.
Submissions may include substitute or alternative items.

Step 6: Requestor Review of Substitute Items
If a supplier submits an alternative or substitute item, the original requestor is notified and must approve or reject the proposed substitute before Procurement may award it.
Only approved substitute items may proceed to supplier awarding.

Step 7: Supplier Evaluation and Awarding
Procurement evaluates supplier quotations based on pricing, compliance, lead time, supplier accreditation status, and approved substitute items.
A winning supplier is selected.

Step 8: PR2 Generation
Procurement generates PR2 using the approved supplier and quotation details.

Step 9: PR2 Approval Routing
Approval Sequence:
Procurement Staff
→ Procurement Manager
→ Director
Upon Director approval, the process proceeds to PO Generation.

Step 10: PO Generation
Procurement generates the Purchase Order based on the approved PR2.

Step 11: PO Approval Routing
Approval Sequence:
Procurement Staff
→ Procurement Manager
→ Finance Director
Upon final approval, the PO becomes available for supplier acknowledgment.

Step 12: Supplier PO Acknowledgment
The supplier acknowledges receipt and acceptance of the Purchase Order.

Step 13: Delivery Tracking
Procurement and Warehouse personnel monitor scheduled, in-transit, delayed, and completed deliveries until physical receipt occurs.

Step 14: GRN Generation and Processing
Warehouse personnel generate the Goods Receipt Note (GRN) and record received quantities, delivery references, receipt dates, and remarks.

Step 15: TSQA Notice for Raw Material Items
For raw material requests, the system displays an informational TSQA notice after GRN processing.
No TSQA workflow, approval routing, queue, status, or system-controlled validation process will be implemented.

Step 16: Procurement Completion
After GRN processing is completed, the procurement request is considered complete.
Completion applies to:
Goods
Services
Raw Materials
The procurement lifecycle concludes at this stage.
